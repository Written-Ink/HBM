My Trucks bookmark for TrackIt

Shows only your trucks on the dispatch map and chimes when one goes Idle on its way back to the plant. It's a bookmark, so it needs no browser extension.

🚚 My Trucks

Drag this button onto your bookmarks bar. Clicking it here does nothing.

  1. Show your bookmarks bar

    Press Ctrl + Shift + B in Chrome or Edge.

  2. Drag the “My Trucks” button onto it

    If dragging doesn’t work, use “Add it by hand” below.

  3. Open the TrackIt map and log in

    trackit-eu.commandalkon.io/dispatch/Map.jsp

  4. Click “My Trucks” on the bookmarks bar

    A panel appears in the top right. Tick your trucks or type their numbers and press Enter. Your choices are remembered.

  5. Click once anywhere on the map

    Browsers only allow sound after a click. Use “Test sound” in the panel to check the volume.

Add it by hand

Copy the code, then right-click the bookmarks bar, choose Add page…, name it My Trucks and paste the code into the URL box.

Click the bookmark again each time you reload or reopen the map. If clicking it does nothing, your IT settings may block bookmarks that run code. In that case, send the details to Claude.
